Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

WombDAO is an open-source research platform. We are committed to collecting as little personal data as possible. This policy explains what information we handle and why.

1. Information We Collect

WombDAO does not require user accounts, registration, or sign-in. We collect only the minimum information necessary to operate the Service:

  • IP addresses — logged temporarily for API rate limiting and abuse prevention. These are not linked to any personal identity.
  • Request metadata — HTTP method, endpoint path, timestamp, and response status code, used for rate limiting and operational monitoring.

We do not collect names, email addresses, payment information, or any other personally identifiable information (PII).

2. API Usage Logs

API requests are logged solely for rate limiting (300 requests / 15 minutes per IP) and for detecting abusive traffic patterns. Logs contain IP addresses, request paths, and timestamps. These logs are rotated and purged automatically and are never sold, shared, or used for analytics or profiling.

3. Cookies

WombDAO does not set any cookies. We do not use tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or any client-side tracking technology. There are no analytics scripts on this site.

4. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with the following third-party services:

  • OpenRouter — The AI agent uses OpenRouter to process chat queries. Your questions are sent to OpenRouter's API for inference. Refer to OpenRouter's privacy policy for details on their data handling.
  • NCBI GEO — Dataset pages link to NCBI GEO entries. Clicking these links takes you to NCBI's site, subject to their own privacy policies.
  • Google Fonts — Font files are loaded from Google's CDN. Google may log requests per their privacy policy.

5. Data Retention

Rate-limiting logs containing IP addresses are retained for a maximum of 24 hours before automatic deletion. We do not maintain long-term records of individual usage. The research datasets themselves are public-domain NCBI GEO data and are retained indefinitely as part of the open archive.

6. Your Rights

Because we collect minimal data and do not maintain user accounts, there is very little personal data to manage. However, you have the right to:

  • Request information about what data, if any, we hold related to your IP address
  • Request deletion of any such data
  • Object to any processing of your data

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the methods below.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is a research platform and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be announced via the project's GitHub repository.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or reach out via X / Twitter.
