Chapter I — Apparatus

Three surfaces. One open laboratory.

Surface 001

Primary source data

156+ ectogenesis datasets sourced from NCBI GEO, indexed and served via a JSON API. Every record links back to its original accession.

Surface 002

A grounded agent, in the browser

Ask questions at /agent. Every answer cites its sources. Toggle agent mode for structured, machine-readable responses.

Surface 003

Governance in public view

The ledger records every proposal and vote. Queryable at the proposals API. No hidden state.

Chapter III — Protocol

Four steps from open tab to contribution.

  1. Open the web agent at /agent and ask a question about ectogenesis.
  2. Review the cited sources — every claim links to its NCBI GEO accession.
  3. Explore the API at /docs to integrate datasets into your own pipeline.
  4. Submit a governance proposal via the ledger to shape the research agenda.
